Curriculum & Literature

The Book Collection

A progressive, culturally rooted learning series developed by Mrs. Chinyere Obianụjụ Bob-Madumere to guide learners through the Igbo language with clarity and confidence.

The Nghọta na Mmụta Asụsụ Igbo series is an Igbo as a Second Language (L2) curriculum crafted by Mrs. Chinyere Obianụjụ Bob-Madumere. Spanning from Primary 1 through Junior Secondary School (JSS 3), it guides pupils, heritage learners, and educators smoothly from basic sounds to expressive, fluent communication.

Ụlọ Akwụkwọ Praịmarị

Primary School Series (Classes 1 – 6)

Carefully graded textbooks for young pupils, designed to build solid phonetics, vocabulary, storytelling, and cultural appreciation from year one through year six.

Primary 1

Akwụkwọ nke Mbụ (Book 1)

Foundational alphabet (Mkpụrụ Edemede), phonics, numbers, family names, and first words for beginners.
