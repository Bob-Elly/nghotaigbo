The award-winning traditional Igbo story on obedience performed by Mrs. Bob-Madumere's children in the national Omenani Igbo Ga Adị competition, demonstrating natural fluency and expression.

Spoken Lesson / Ọnụọgụgụ

Ọnụọgụgụ Igbo (Igbo Numbers 1–20)

Clear, step-by-step counting from 1 to 20 in Igbo. Teaches accurate tone inflections and pronunciation, making number learning intuitive and memorable.

Mkpụrụedemede Igbo (The ABC Fun Way)

Mastering the 36 letters of the Igbo alphabet the fun, musical way. A signature Nghotaigbo creation that helps children and beginners pronounce every sound correctly.
