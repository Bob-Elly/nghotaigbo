Onye Ka Anyị Bụ? • Who We Are

Leaving the Frustration & Blame Behind

We understand the deep desire to speak your mother tongue and pass it on to your children. You are not alone on this journey.

💭

Have You Felt This?

Have you ever felt frustrated after learning a particular Igbo word, only to forget it when standing in front of a native speaker? Do you dream of speaking Igbo fluently or raising children who can confidently communicate in Igbo, only to meet a dead end?

💔

The Roadblock

Many parents have bought books, hired tutors, and made efforts, only to discover that their efforts were not yielding the desired results. The pain has led some to resign to fate, or blame spouses and relatives. But it is time to leave the blame game behind.

🌱

Our Commitment

We are here at NGHOTAIGBO to assist, inspire, and challenge you so that your dream of speaking and teaching the Igbo language can become a reality. We walk with you step by step until confidence takes root.

Our Mission

“NGHOTAIGBO Educational Services Limited is an organisation that provides excellent educational resources, enabling Igbo teachers and learners to teach and learn the Igbo language with ease.”

Our desire is to inspire, encourage, and support Igbo learners and parents who want to learn Igbo or teach it to their children but do not know how to go about it.
